# Shukti's Romhack - Complete Gameplay Walkthrough

Welcome to the definitive walkthrough for Shukti's special birthday edition of **Pokémon FireRed Ultra**. This guide is designed to walk you through the entire main story, explain all gym battles and major bosses, and detail how to complete every single side quest.

---

## 🚀 Beginning Your Journey (Clear Town)

Your adventure begins in **Clear Town** (formerly Pallet Town).
- **Identity & Auto-Intro**: The typical tutorial from Professor Tree is bypassed. You start directly in your bedroom as **SHUKTI** (Female protagonist).
- **Birthday Wishes**: Interact with the PC in your room to read a custom birthday email from your gym guide **Ollie**.
- **Starters**: Go downstairs, say goodbye to your Mom (**Niky**) and Stepdad (**Gary**), and head outside. Professor Tree will stop you at the tall grass. Head to the lab to choose one of three buffed starter Pokémon:
  - **Morpeko** (Electric/Dark) - Neon Green theme, has buffed Ultra stats.
  - **Eevee** (Normal) - Flexible evolutions, has buffed Ultra stats.
  - **Alolan Raichu** (Electric/Psychic) - Highly powerful Special Attacker.
- **Clear Town Stones**: Make sure to scour Clear Town before leaving! There are several evolution stones hidden around the town to help you evolve Eevee early.

## 🗺️ Main Story Walkthrough (29 steps)

The Start Menu features a custom **Quest Log** to track your main story progress step-by-step:

- **Step 0**: *Go downstairs and talk to your Mom. She's probably worried about you.*
  - **How to advance**: Speak to Niky on the 1F of your home.
- **Step 1**: *Go find Professor Tree. He's usually wandering near Clear Town's exit.*
  - **How to advance**: Walk towards the north exit of Clear Town.
- **Step 2**: *Deliver the Special Edition Twilight Boxed Set to Professor Tree.*
  - **How to advance**: Retrieve the parcel (Twilight Boxed Set) from the Adrenaline City Mart clerk and return it to Tree in his lab.
- **Step 3**: *Get the POKéDEX from Professor Tree and head to Pavement City.*
  - **How to advance**: Receive the Pokédex from Tree at the end of the lab scene.
- **Step 4**: *Defeat Faris at the Pavement City Gym to earn your first badge.*
  - **How to advance**: Go north to Adrenaline City, east through Route 2, and enter Pavement City (formerly Pewter City). Defeat **Faris** to receive the **PAVEMENTBADGE** and the **Poo Bag** (anti-Rare Candy level decreaser).
- **Step 5**: *Go through Mt. Moon and head to Poolside City.*
  - **How to advance**: Travel east from Pavement City, traverse Mt. Moon, and arrive in Poolside City (formerly Cerulean City).
- **Step 6**: *Defeat Bryony at the Poolside City Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Bryony** in the Poolside City Gym to receive the **POOLSIDEBADGE**. (Note: Look out for the legendary pug **Bowie** inside the gym!).
- **Step 7**: *Visit BILL at the Sea Cottage on Route 25.*
  - **How to advance**: Go north through Nugget Bridge, proceed east to Route 25, and enter Bill's cottage. Help him transform back into a human.
- **Step 8**: *Get the S.S. TICKET and head to Burnout City.*
  - **How to advance**: Speak to Bill after helping him to receive the S.S. Ticket, then travel south through the Underground Path to Burnout City (formerly Vermilion City).
- **Step 9**: *Defeat LT. SURGE at the Burnout City Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Hrishi** (Lt. Surge's replacement) in the Burnout City Gym to earn the **BURNOUTBADGE**.
- **Step 10**: *Get HM01 (CUT) from the S.S. ANNE Captain.*
  - **How to advance**: Board the cruise ship **The Royal Princess** (formerly S.S. Anne) in Burnout harbor and talk to the seasick Captain.
- **Step 11**: *Go to Empire City and investigate the ROCKET GAME CORNER.*
  - **How to advance**: Use Cut to reach Route 9, traverse Rock Tunnel, and enter Empire City (formerly Celadon City). Find the Rocket poster in the Game Corner and press the switch.
- **Step 12**: *Defeat DAVID at the Empire City Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **David** (bodybuilder Bruno sprite) at the Empire City Gym to receive the **EMPIREBADGE** and the **Protein Bar** level-increaser.
- **Step 13**: *Rescue the SILPH CO. President in Starrider City.*
  - **How to advance**: Enter Saffron/Starrider City, navigate the warp tiles in Silph Co., defeat the Rocket Boss on 11F, and talk to the President.
- **Step 14**: *Defeat SABRINA at the Starrider City Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Diva** (Sabrina's replacement) in the Starrider Gym to receive the **STARRIDERBADGE**.
- **Step 15**: *Go to Hustler City through the CYCLING ROAD.*
  - **How to advance**: Head west from Empire City, travel down the Cycling Road, and enter Hustler City (formerly Fuchsia City).
- **Step 16**: *Defeat KOGA at the Hustler City Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Milan** (Koga's replacement) in the Hustler City Gym to receive the **HUSTLERBADGE**.
- **Step 17**: *Explore the POKéMON MANSION on Diablo Island.*
  - **How to advance**: Surf south to Diablo Island (formerly Cinnabar Island) and search the Pokémon Mansion for the Secret Key.
- **Step 18**: *Defeat BLAINE at Diablo Island Gym.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Devang** (Blaine's replacement) in the Diablo Island Gym to receive the **DIABLOBADGE**.
- **Step 19**: *Go to Adrenaline City for your final Gym Challenge.*
  - **How to advance**: Return to Adrenaline City (formerly Viridian City) to face the final Gym.
- **Step 20**: *Defeat the Final Gym Leader in Adrenaline City.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat Gym Leaders **Zaynab & Aliyah** in their double battle Gym to earn the final **ADRENALINEBADGE**.
- **Step 21**: *Challenge the ELITE FOUR at the Libertine Plateau!*
  - **How to advance**: Head west from Adrenaline City through Route 22, pass all badge checkpoints, cross Victory Road, and enter the League building at the Libertine Plateau (formerly Indigo Plateau).
- **Step 22**: *Defeat LORELEI of the ELITE FOUR.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Nick** (Lorelei replacement).
- **Step 23**: *Defeat BRUNO of the ELITE FOUR.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Andrea** (Bruno replacement).
- **Step 24**: *Defeat JACQUIE of the ELITE FOUR.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat **Jacquie** (Agatha replacement, Inept/iPad theme, uses underleveled Ice team).
- **Step 25**: *Defeat LANCE of the ELITE FOUR.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat Lance.
- **Step 26**: *Defeat the CHAMPION!*
  - **How to advance**: Face and defeat **Ollie** (your gym guide, Steven Stone sprite) in the final Champion battle to enter the Hall of Fame.
- **Step 27**: *Visit CELIO at the POKéMON CENTER on ONE ISLAND.*
  - **How to advance**: Take the ferry to One Island and talk to Celio in the Pokémon Center.
- **Step 28**: *Recover the RUBY and SAPPHIRE gems from the SEVII ISLANDS.*
  - **How to advance**: Defeat the Rocket grunts on Mt. Ember, navigate the Icefall Cave on Four Island, complete the Ruin Valley puzzle on Six Island, retrieve the gems, and return them to Celio.
- **Step 29**: *Confront MEWTWO in the depths of the POOLSIDE CAVE.*
  - **How to advance**: Head to Poolside City, Surf into the Poolside Cave (formerly Cerulean Cave), and defeat/catch Mewtwo at the bottom.

## 📋 Side Quest Walkthroughs

Apart from the Main Story, there are 7 custom side quests you can track and complete:

### 1. Stacey's Redundancy
Your old boss Stacey is causing trouble across Kanto with failed business strategies. Find her and clean up her messes:
1. **Poolside City (Berry Bakery)**: Enter the house at the top-left of Poolside City. Speak to Stacey, who is running a bakery. Sabotage her oven by having a Pokémon in your party with a **WATER-type move**. 
   - *Reward*: `ITEM_CHARCOAL`.
2. **Masquerade Town (Spiritual Consultancy)**: Enter Mr. Fuji's house in Masquerade Town. Speak to Stacey, who has set up a spiritual consulting booth. Scare her by showing her a **GHOST-type Pokémon** in your party.
   - *Reward*: `ITEM_SPELL_TAG`.
3. **Diablo Island (Volcano Insurance)**: Speak to Stacey in the Lab Lounge on Diablo Island. She is selling volcano insurance. Survive a Risk Assessment battle against Hiker Dudley.
   - *Reward*: `ITEM_LAVA_COOKIE`.

### 2. The Rescue of FIG
Team Rocket has kidnapped Fig (a special Shiny Rockruff). Retrace her steps and rescue her:
1. **Route 2**: Find **FIG'S TREATS** on the ground (looks like an item ball).
2. **Route 9**: Find the **SHINY FUR CLUMP** on the ground.
3. **Sighting Clues**: Speak to NPCs in Pavement City ("thugs in black suits dragging a crate to Mt. Moon"), Route 3 ("dog bit one of those Rocket guys"), and Burnout City ("heading to the Underground Path with a heavy bag") for clues.
4. **Rescue**: Infiltrate Silph Co. in Starrider City. Defeat the Rocket Boss on 11F. The President will thank you by returning FIG to your party!

### 3. Twilight Trivia Hunt
Professor Tree is a massive Twilight fan (Team Edward) but has lost his boxed set chapters. Find all 4 chapters:
1. **Chapter 1**: Located on 2F of the Pokémon Tower (Masquerade Town).
2. **Chapter 2**: Located inside the Game Corner (Empire City).
3. **Chapter 3**: Located on 11F of Silph Co. (Starrider City).
4. **Chapter 4**: Located on B1F of the Pokémon Mansion (Diablo Island).
   - *Rewards*: Return each chapter to Professor Tree in his lab for a **Rare Candy** (4 total).

### 4. PokeASDA Mystery Shopper
Audit Marts for PokeASDA to earn a permanent global discount:
1. Speak to the clerk in the **Pavement City** PokeASDA Mart to perform the first audit.
2. Speak to the clerk in the **Poolside City** PokeASDA Mart to perform the second audit.
3. Head to the PokeASDA HQ on the 1F of the Empire City Dept. Store. Speak to the Receptionist to activate your **20% Staff Discount** globally!

### 5. The Professional Palette
Show the Hairdresser in Clear Town (the fat man on the east side of town) specific Pokémon matching the Danger Jones hair color palette:
1. **Neon Green**: Show a Victreebel.
2. **Fiery Red**: Show a Flareon.
3. **Masquerade Purple**: Show a Gengar.
   - *Reward*: `ITEM_SHINY_STONE`.

### 6. Eevee Adoption Program
Adopt 10 scattered Eevees from Mart clerks to save them. Eevee givers are located in the PokeASDA Marts of:
1. **Adrenaline City**
2. **Pavement City**
3. **Poolside City**
4. **Masquerade Town**
5. **Burnout City**
6. **Empire City**
7. **Hustler City**
8. **Starrider City**
9. **Diablo Island**
10. **One Island** Pokémon Center
    - *Reward*: Adopt all 10 Eevees to receive an **EVERSTONE**.

### 7. Birthday Party Invite (Post-Game)
Once you have entered the Hall of Fame as the Champion, you must find and invite 8 of your friends hidden across Kanto to your birthday party:
1. **Claire**: Located in Adrenaline City.
2. **Steve**: Located in Pavement City.
3. **Fernanda**: Located in Poolside City.
4. **Sam**: Located in Masquerade Town.
5. **Joe**: Located in Burnout City.
6. **Zaynab**: Located in Empire City.
7. **Aliyah**: Located in Hustler City.
8. **Aditi**: Located in Diablo Island.
   - **Grand Finale**: Once all 8 are invited, head back to your bedroom in Clear Town and interact with your PC. The PC will glitch out and warp you to the secret **Birthday Party Room** for a surprise celebration with all your friends!
